# Phase 3.9a — allowed contract and scene-first debt planning

Read the actual per-text contract before drafting. Only ALLOWED_CORE,
ACTIVE_EXPANSION, ALLOWED_NEW, registered names and frozen exceptions are
permitted. FORBIDDEN_FUTURE is a negative list, never a source of synonyms.
Natural-sounding unintroduced words are still forbidden. Planned dates do not
prove introduction; only this run's committed state does.

Choose one simple goal using the planned new words and at most three scene
proposals. Consider compatible debt bundles early, before the last quarter of
their window. Deadline pressure never overrides a semantic veto. Do not use
every suggested word. Aim for 8–12 actual distinct Expansion appearances when
natural; early texts with few introduced words cannot meet this soft target.
Avoid repeating only already-capped common words at the expense of naturally
usable under-exposed words. Frozen actual window payment remains authoritative.

Draft about 130–150 eojeol, 3–7 per sentence, maximum 10; natural short replies
are allowed. Repetition of known nouns and Core words is welcome. Introduce each
new lemma once, or 2+1 only when previous coverage stays at least 98%. No filler
adverbs, literary synonyms, harder constructions or review-only side events.

Bad-pattern memory: N에게 웃어요; 책을 열어요 for a paper book; 자전거를 닫아요;
가방을 만들어요 when packing; an unavailable action replaced by a near verb;
ownership confusion; switching anonymous 그 사람; 좋아하다 as a universal reaction;
unnecessary 의; habitual -고 있다; literal Japanese noun relations; abrupt subject
changes. Omit, describe the result, change the scene, or use an existing natural
expression. Never invent an unnatural approximation. Missing 펴다: read the book
without describing opening it. Missing 입히다: omit dressing somebody else.
Missing 지우다: omit erasing, or naturally write again; do not substitute 닦다.

Before returning the first draft, check each content word against the compact
allowed contract, the independent title, planned new presence, known gaps and
referents. Save this author self-check separately. It is not the mechanical gate.
Do not silently perform official preflight scans without recording findings.

아침 includes morning/breakfast; 저녁 includes evening/dinner under their existing
IDs. Respect the attached explicit restrictions; do not infer other sense
expansions. Semantic grammar classifications are diagnostics only.

Observed gap additions only: when 불다 is unavailable, omit wind instead of
밖에는 비하고 바람이 있어요; e.g. 밖에는 비가 많이 와요. For a written message,
do not force 말을 as the object: 종이에 써요, followed by the direct message,
is preferable. These are author strategies, not new hard semantic rules.

Self-check output MUST use config/languages/ko/k1/author_selfcheck.schema.json.

then record only checks actually performed. Do not invent alternative keys.
The per-text contract includes the same schema path and generated template.
If schema validation fails, preserve Korean, Japanese and title exactly and
repair only self-check JSON. Up to three failed schema submissions are allowed;
these have no formal candidate attempt and no actual state credit. Complete
contract repair first; ordinary prose repair is a separate later step.
